**What's new in this version**

- **Fixed: "Oh dear, you cannot sell this item on Etsy" when using the newer era options.** The last update added recent eras (Made to order, 2020–2026, 2010–2019, 2007–2009), but Etsy rejects those for a resold item made by another company — an item has to be handmade, vintage (20+ years old), or a craft supply. Step 3 now has two new questions, **"Who made it?"** and **"What is it?"**, so you can correctly list handmade items and craft supplies with any era. If you're reselling vintage, leave them on the defaults ("Another company or person" / "A finished product") and pick a vintage era exactly like before.
- **The app now catches this before sending to Etsy.** If a combination isn't allowed (e.g. a newer item marked as made by someone else and not a supply), you'll get a clear, plain-English explanation on the item-details step telling you how to fix it — instead of a confusing rejection after the fact.
