**What's new in this version**

- **Step 5 now lets you search Etsy categories by name.** If the AI didn't auto-pick an Etsy category and you didn't set one on Step 4, the final step used to ask for a numeric category ID. It now shows the same search-by-name picker as Step 4 — type "book" or "mug" or "planter" and tap the matching category. No more needing to know Etsy's internal ID numbers.
