**What's new in this version**

- **Fixed: Etsy drafts failing with "& can only be used once" (and similar invalid-character errors).** Etsy only allows the characters `&`, `%`, `:`, and `+` once each in a listing title, and rejects certain symbols (like `$`, `^`, emoji) entirely. If your title used one of these more than once — for example "M&M ... Cookie & Candy Jar" — the draft would be refused and nothing was created. The app now automatically tidies the title to meet Etsy's rules before sending (a second `&` becomes "and", a second `+` becomes "plus", and unsupported symbols are removed), so the draft goes through. The AI is also guided to avoid these characters when it writes titles.
- **Better failure diagnostics.** When an Etsy draft is rejected, the support log now records the exact title and fields that were sent, making problems like this much faster to pin down.
